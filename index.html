Summary Formulas

Total Daily Sodium Requirement (mmol/day):

$$ \text{Sodium (mmol/kg/day)} \times \text{Weight (kg)} $$

Total Daily Potassium Requirement (mmol/day):

$$ \text{Potassium (mmol/kg/day)} \times \text{Weight (kg)} $$

Total Fluid Requirement (mL/day):

$$ \text{Fluid (mL/kg/day)} \times \text{Weight (kg)} $$

Infusion Rate (mL/hr):

$$ \text{Total Fluid (mL)} \div 24 $$

Total Sodium Needed (mmol):

$$ \left( \frac{\text{Daily Sodium}}{\text{Daily Fluid}} \right) \times 500 $$

Total Potassium Needed (mmol):

$$ \left( \frac{\text{Daily Potassium}}{\text{Daily Fluid}} \right) \times 500 $$
